FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Key details regarding team composition, fees, accommodation, and evaluation rules.

As explicitly mandated in the official poster, each team must consist of exactly 4 members. Individual submissions or teams with less than or more than 4 members are not permitted.

The ₹1,000 registration fee per team covers participation for all 4 team members, full catering during the 24-hour hackathon (breakfast, lunch, dinner, high-energy midnight snacks, and coffee), Wi-Fi lab access, official hackathon kit/swag, and verified merit certificates.

The Screening Round occurs on September 26 - 27, 2026, where teams present their idea, approach, and system design. Shortlisted squads then enter the 24-Hour Physical Hackathon on October 10 - 11, 2026, at VIIT CSE labs to build and pitch the working prototype.

Yes! Samartha welcomes engineering students from CSE, IT, ECE, AI/ML, and interdisciplinary streams across approved colleges and universities. Diverse teams with members skilled in AI, backend, frontend, and design are strongly encouraged.
